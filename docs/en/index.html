Why do SRT subtitles flash 1-frame black gaps in Premiere Pro & DaVinci Resolve?

Most ASR exporters write arbitrary milliseconds (e.g., 00:00:02,213) that sit mid-frame. At 24fps (41.67ms) or 23.976fps (41.71ms), independent rounding of in and out points leaves 1-frame holes or overlaps. Quantizing to exact integer frames fixes this permanently.

When should I use the free Browser Studio vs. the paid AI Agent Skill?

Use the free Browser Studio on this page whenever you already have a synced .srt and need 9:16 vertical splitting, 23.976↔24fps drift repair, or Final Cut Pro .fcpxml export. Use the full AI Skill when aligning raw video/audio against a plain script from scratch.
